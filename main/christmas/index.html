Before you order

Christmas ordering: three things to know

Order early

Place orders before the first week of December. Buying-platform warehouses slow down badly in mid-December.

Ask for QC photos

Request QC photos before international shipping and check stitching, size tags and hardware.

Pick the right line

Express costs more and arrives faster; standard lines are cheaper and slower. Decide before you order.

FAQ

Christmas gift FAQ

When should I order Christmas gifts?
Place orders in November if you can. Early December still works on express lines; after mid-December, delivery is a gamble.
Do you ship straight to the recipient?
No. Orders go to the buying-platform warehouse first. You approve QC photos, then forward internationally.
Are these original brand products?
No. These are style-led pieces inspired by popular silhouettes; we do not claim brand authenticity.
What if the size is wrong?
Check the size chart and ask for measurements in QC. Sneakers run true to size; oversized hoodies run large.
Can I use this list for Black Friday?
Yes. The same picks work for Black Friday and Cyber Monday — stock moves fastest in the last week of November.
